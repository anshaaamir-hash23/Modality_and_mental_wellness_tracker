✨ Modality Tracker

Project Overview: This application tracks user interactions across different input modalities (Mouse Clicks & Keyboard Keypresses) to record daily mental wellness reflections into an interactive star constellation canvas.

Select your mood and type your reflection. Every entry turns into a glowing star in the night sky!

🔒 Privacy Notice: Gesture & Eye-tracking features are disabled to ensure complete camera privacy and security.
